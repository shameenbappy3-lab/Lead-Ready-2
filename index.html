How it works

Follow the lead from the inbox to a booked visit.

No jargon. This is what actually happens for your customer.

  1. Step 1

    A lead lands in your email

    Someone fills out your website, replies to an ad, or emails you. Same idea for a leaky pipe, an overgrown yard, a flooded basement, or a family looking for in-home care.

  2. Step 2

    Their details are pulled out automatically

    Name, phone number, and what they need — a lawn cleanup, a broken water line, weekday help at home — are read from the message so nobody has to type it in by hand.

  3. Step 3

    They get an email and a text right away

    The lead is added to your list. They hear from your business immediately — so they know a real company got the request, not a black hole.

  4. Step 4

    A polite assistant calls them

    A natural-sounding voice calls using their name and the job they asked for. First question: are they free to talk about a visit right now?

  5. Step 5

    Two paths: talk now, or pick a time

    If they’re available, the call is handed to your phone so you can close it yourself. If not, a time is set for later — no phone tag.

  6. Step 6

    You get a clear note if a visit was booked

    After the call, if an appointment was set, a short summary and the time land in your email. You show up prepared — name, job, and when.
